# v1.6 | 2026-11-24
MXS Remastered v1.6 OUT NOW - Lap timer, setup tab, rider/bike dirt, and more

## What's new?
- **Lap timer:** Shows your lap, current time, predicted time, checkpoint and the live gap to your best lap. You can drag it, resize it and pin it (in "Other" tab)
- **Setups tab:** Load, save, rename and delete your bike setups from the mod menu
- **Setup sharing:** Copy a setup as a code and send it to anyone (your name, number, gear, and controls are not affected)
- **Bike dirt:** Mud and dust on your bike (adjustable)
- **Rider dirt:** Mud and dust on your gear (adjustable)
- New 250 and 125 2t sounds: Not final, probably going to have an update specifically for sounds
- Better looking tire and roost dirt
- Ground brightness slider: Brightens only the ground, mostly the dark berms and jump faces
- Separate toggles for bike & rider shadows, object shadows and ground shading (helps with performance)
- Preset codes: "Copy preset" and "Paste preset" buttons now work from any tab
- Menu remembers the size you set
- Better performance with detailed shadows in large lobbies

## First person updates
- Lean limiter slider: Helps with stability by cutting down how much the camera moves when you lean forward/back
- Arm fade slider: Fades out your arms in first person from the shoulders down which allows for higher FOVs without shoulders getting in the way (works with shaders off too)

## Bug fixes
- Fixed invisible trees
- Fixed weird horizon lines
- Fixed stadium shadows flickering and being patchy
- Fixed roost and water spray happening while in the air
- Tire marks now clear when the track reloads
- Detailed shadows optimization

# v1.0 | 2026-11-04
INTRODUCING MXS REMASTERED - NOW APPROVED BY rF
I'M MAKING IT COMPLETELY FREE FOR EVERYBODY, ALL THESE MFS TRYNA MAKE MONEY

## First Person Camera (includes everything from MXCam + more)
- Dynamic first person view that rides with the bike instead of being stuck to it
- Stabilization: horizon lock, pitch lock, steadiness control
- Neck and G-forces: your head compresses on landings and nods with impacts
- Look to apex: your head turns into corners (currently buggy)
- Controller d-pad look left, right, and behind
- Air control: looser view in the air, with an optional whip lock that keeps the view on your line during whips and scrubs
- Adjustable head position, look-down angle, and smoothing, plus an option to hide your torso and head at high FOVs

## Replay/Spectating Cameras
- 3rd person chase cam (locked view in the works)
- Helmet cam: top, left side, right side, or chest mount, with GoPro-style stabilization

## Shaders
- Time of day: a full day/night cycle with sunsets, stars, and a moonlit nights and the sun lighting the bikes from where it is
- Bike headlight for night riding (currently buggy in 3rd person)
- Real-time shadows from bikes, riders, trees and track objects, plus ambient occlusion
- Tire marks: your tires dig ruts into the track (currently buggy)
- Tire dirt: mud builds up on your tires (currently buggy)
- Dirt detail: high-res grain, pebbles, and clods on the track up close
- Sun rays, bloom, depth of field, motion blur
- Sharper image: anti-aliasing (FXAA), sharpening, anisotropic and trilinear filtering
- Color controls: brightness, contrast, saturation, vignette

## Easy to use
- Pull up the in-game menu with (E)
- Hotkeys (changeable in the keybinds tab): F8 toggles the camera mod and F7 toggles the shaders
- Only want the shaders? Turn the camera mod off and keep the graphics
